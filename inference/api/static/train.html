About these scores

Sexism score — proportion of the 6 annotators who labeled the meme as sexist. A score of 1.0 means unanimous agreement.

Direct score — among annotators who said sexist, proportion who labeled it as DIRECT (vs JUDGEMENTAL).

Category scores — proportion of annotators who assigned each category. Multi-label: a meme can have multiple categories active.
